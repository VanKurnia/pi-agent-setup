Scan Scratchpad/ for notes older than 14 days. For each:
- Describes implemented work? → belongs in Projects/{Category}/. Add `created` + `modified` frontmatter on promotion.
- Still in progress? → leave in Scratchpad
- Obsolete? → move to Ideas/Archived/
- Fleeting thought with no follow-up? → delete or move to Ideas/

Output a promotion plan. Do not move or delete any files — just report.
